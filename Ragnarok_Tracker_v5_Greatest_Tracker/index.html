Quick guide

Getting Started 🧭

The tracker is designed so you can start checking quests immediately. These are the main tools to know.

1Choose your characterCreate or switch characters from the character bar. Each character keeps separate Daily and Weekly progress.
2Track Dailies & WeekliesCheck quests as you finish them. Reset timing follows the game server: 5:00 AM UTC+7, with Weeklies resetting Monday.
3Use the Adventure JournalSave cards, equipment, Trade House targets, achievements, and long-term goals. Journal items do not reset automatically.
4Optional Cloud SyncPair another device only if you want phone ↔ PC syncing. Keep your pairing code private like a password.
5Send FeedbackUse Send Feedback for bugs or suggestions. Reports are private and are not visible to other players.
